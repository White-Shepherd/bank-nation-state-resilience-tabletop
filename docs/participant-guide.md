# Participant guide

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK**

Act within your role, state assumptions, challenge uncertain facts, identify dissent, and make decisions with the information available. Focus on customer harm, payments, liquidity, integrity, minimum viable service, trustworthy recovery, third parties, and communication. Do not assume backups, replication, failover, or system availability are trustworthy. Do not infer actor attribution from a single anomaly. Notification timing and legal conclusions belong to counsel/compliance.
