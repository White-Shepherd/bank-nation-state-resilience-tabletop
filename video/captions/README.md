# Caption production

Generate captions after picture lock. Use WebVTT, sentence case, two lines maximum, approximately 32–42 characters per line, and at least one second per cue where speech permits. Preserve the exact terms “maximum tolerable disruption (MTD),” “recovery time objective (RTO),” “recovery point objective (RPO),” and “Tier 0 candidate.”

Every video begins with: “All organizations, systems, evidence, events, and values shown are fictional and synthetic.” Do not omit spoken qualifications such as “candidate,” “may,” “declared,” “unvalidated,” or “requires human approval.” Identify speakers only when needed. Proof captions against the final audio and on-screen text; do not cover application controls.
