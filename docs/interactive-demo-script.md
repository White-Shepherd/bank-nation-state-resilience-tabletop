# Interactive demonstration script

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK**

1. Open **Resilience Ecosystem Map** and select **Wire transfers**.
2. In the explorer, expand and explain payment initiation, approval, sanctions, fraud review, posting, and reconciliation.
3. Reveal the wire platform, payment gateway, transaction journal, and reconciliation database.
4. Switch to Technical view and show Tier 0 identity, privileged access, certificates, database management, telecommunications, and recovery control planes.
5. Open Tool coverage and distinguish deployed, telemetry, tested detection, response integration, and recovery visibility.
6. Move scenario time to Ambiguous pre-positioning to reveal the identity and certificate inject.
7. Show Identity Team, Security Operations, CISO, Incident Commander, and escalation to the CEO.
8. Record the containment decision in the existing Decisions tab: isolate production identity and invoke independent recovery access.
9. Move to Containment dilemma and show the wire outage/manual-processing consequence.
10. Move to Recovery and show clean-room prerequisites, reconciliation, and minimum viable restoration.
11. Open Investment view and select independent recovery identity, clean-room recovery, transaction-integrity validation, and third-party substitution for board consideration.

Close by explaining that the map translates technical dependencies into customer, payment, liquidity, integrity, decision, and investment consequences.
