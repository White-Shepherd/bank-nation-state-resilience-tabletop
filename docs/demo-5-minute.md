# Five-minute portfolio demonstration

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK**

**0:00 - Geopolitical risk.** Explain that strategic warning becomes ambiguous control-plane activity and coordinated service disruption. Emphasize defensive uncertainty and no unsupported attribution.

**0:40 - Critical services.** Show the 16 services and the distinction between RTO and MTD. Point out payment, liquidity, customer-harm, backlog, integrity, and minimum viable service tolerances.

**1:20 - Tier 0.** Open Enterprise Identity. Show how one dependency spans services and recovery, satisfies explicit rules, carries evidence, and still requires human approval. Distinguish the local designation from NIST CSF Implementation Tiers.

**2:05 - Warning indicators.** Reveal identity, certificate, backup, carrier, and reconciliation evidence. Explain why correlation supports a decision while no single signal proves a nation-state actor.

**2:45 - Difficult decision.** In Phase 4, ask whether to isolate suspected identity when payment operations may stop. Record assumptions, dissent, consequences, reversibility, evidence, and accepted residual risk.

**3:35 - Board dashboard.** Show services affected, explicit tolerance consumption, integrity and recovery confidence, Tier 0 exposure, decision deadlines, and why raw alert count is absent.

**4:20 - Investment recommendation.** Compare prevention, detection, redundancy, clean-room recovery, concentration reduction, transfer, and redesign. Recommend removing recovery common modes first, with accountable milestones and quarterly evidence.

Close: “This project translates technical dependencies into business-risk decisions—who is harmed, which obligation is threatened, what evidence is trustworthy, and what the board must fund or accept.”
