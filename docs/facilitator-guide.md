# Facilitator guide

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK**

Before the session, validate participant authority, tolerances, dependency maps, alternate communications, decision logging, and safety. Remind participants that all evidence is synthetic and attribution is unresolved. Run 10 minutes of orientation, the 240-minute scenario with breaks absorbed into phase transitions, and a 20-minute hotwash if schedule permits.

Reveal only the selected injects. Ask: What service and tolerance is at risk? What evidence changes confidence? Is the action reversible? Could availability conceal corruption? Does recovery share the suspect control plane? Who accepts residual risk? Record facts, assumptions, dissent, deadlines, decisions, and counterfactuals. Never invent participant performance. Stop if anyone introduces real confidential data.

Afterward, validate observations with participants; assign owners/dates/acceptance criteria; distinguish contradictions from incomplete evidence; and route jurisdiction-dependent legal claims to counsel. Generate the packet and AAR, then retain according to the fictional exercise policy.
