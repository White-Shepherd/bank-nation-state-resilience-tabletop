# Framework and regulatory considerations

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK. Counsel and compliance must determine applicability and notification timing.**

| Source | Type | Exercise use |
|---|---|---|
| [NIST CSF 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) | Industry framework | Govern, identify services/dependencies, protect, detect, respond, recover |
| [NIST SP 800-61r3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) | Recommended federal practice | Integrate incident response with CSF 2.0 risk management |
| [FFIEC Business Continuity Management booklet](https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/business-continuity-management.aspx) | Interagency examination guidance | BIA, resilience, testing, third-party and recovery considerations |
| [Sound Practices to Strengthen Operational Resilience](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20201030a.htm) | Federal banking supervisory guidance | Useful exercise framing only; the published scope does not automatically include this fictional $35B bank |
| [CISA Cross-Sector CPGs](https://www.cisa.gov/cross-sector-cybersecurity-performance-goals) | Recommended practice | High-impact baseline safeguards |
| [MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise](https://attack.mitre.org/) | Industry knowledge base | Defensive behavioral coverage, not attribution |

Actual regulatory requirements depend on charter, regulator, facts, contracts, laws, and jurisdiction. The scenario's institution profile, tolerances, materiality discussions, Tier 0 label, RAG rules, and notification placeholders are exercise assumptions—not legal conclusions.
