# Impact-Tolerance Guide

Assess each impact dimension independently at 15 minutes, 1, 4, 8, 24 and 72 hours, and 7 days using: 1 negligible, 2 limited, 3 material, 4 high, 5 severe. High/severe entries require narrative rationale. Review RTO versus maximum disruption, RPO versus data uncertainty, workaround duration versus recovery, minimum viable service, approval, and testing. Conflicts are flagged, never silently corrected.
