# Continuum Resilience Visual Standards

Continuum Resilience positions the platform as **Cyber Operational Resilience Decision Intelligence**. Harbor Ridge Bank remains a fictional demonstration client. No trademark availability claim is made for any candidate name.

## Naming decision

| Candidate | Clarity | Credibility | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuum Resilience | High | High | Selected. Connects sustained service, recovery and operational continuity. |
| ControlPoint Intelligence | High | Medium-high | Technically clear but can imply a narrow security product. |
| Assurance Vector | Medium | High | Credible but requires more explanation. |

## Wordmark and lockup

The primary wordmark uses **CONTINUUM** in Aptos Display Bold with **RESILIENCE** aligned below in Signal Teal. The compact wordmark is the `CR` monogram separated by a vertical Signal Teal rule. The presentation lockup pairs the wordmark with the descriptor.

- Safe area: preserve clear space equal to the capital `C` height on all sides.
- Minimum primary wordmark width: 1.6 inches in presentations, 180 px in digital use.
- Minimum compact mark: 0.32 inches or 32 px.
- Light backgrounds: Continuum Navy wordmark with Signal Teal accent.
- Dark backgrounds: white wordmark with Signal Teal accent.
- Monochrome: all Continuum Navy on light backgrounds or all white on dark backgrounds.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, recolor individual letters, add shadows, place on low-contrast imagery, or combine with the Harbor Ridge name as though it were the product.

## Typography and grid

Aptos is the primary PowerPoint font because it is standard on current Microsoft 365 installations. Arial is the explicit fallback for older Windows environments. Use a 12-column, 24 px gutter grid on a 1280 by 720 canvas with 72 px outer margins. Titles use 36 px or larger, body copy 18 px, and labels 13 px. Shorten copy before reducing type.

## Spacing and lines

Use an 8 px base spacing scale: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 72. Hairlines are 1 px, standard dividers 2 px and emphasis rules 4 px. Corners are square or subtly rounded; avoid pill shapes.

## Visual grammar

Each slide makes one claim. Evidence is Slate Blue, uncertainty is muted violet-gray, findings use Continuum Navy, recommendations use Signal Teal, board decisions use Copper, and exceptions use semantic red or amber with a written status label. Do not rely on color alone.

## Icons, photography and illustration

Use simple line icons only when they encode a known action or object; never decorate empty space with icons. Avoid hacker imagery, padlocks, neon effects, generic stock photography and meaningless technology abstractions. Photography, when necessary, should document real operational environments without revealing sensitive architecture. Illustrations use flat geometry, controlled line weight and labeled causality.

## Accessibility

Maintain at least 4.5:1 text contrast, minimum 16 px body text, descriptive alternative text, direct chart labels, patterns or text status markers, logical reading order, and short labels supported by speaker notes or accessible detail.
