# Video 3 — Dependencies and Tier 0

**Opening hook:** “A critical service can have dozens of components, but only a few dependencies hold concentrated authority over many of them.”

**Audience outcome:** Understand declared dependency mapping, common mode, candidate rules, and approval boundaries. **Required state:** checkpoints 4–6. **Expected runtime:** 7:15.
