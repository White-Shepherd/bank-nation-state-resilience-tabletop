# Board risk framework

**SYNTHETIC EXERCISE DATA - NOT A REAL BANK**

## Risk statement

Because Harbor Ridge Bank depends on concentrated identity, payment, telecommunications and recovery control planes, a capable nation-state actor could disrupt or corrupt multiple critical banking services, resulting in customer harm, missed financial obligations, liquidity pressure, regulatory consequences and prolonged recovery.

## Synthetic appetite statements

The board expects each critical service to remain within its approved MTD and payment deadlines; zero tolerance applies to knowingly posting unreconciled balances as authoritative. Restoration requires independently validated identity, configuration, and recovery-point integrity. Single-provider dependencies require tested substitution or explicit acceptance. Manual capacity must sustain the service's stated minimum level for its documented duration. Unvalidated recovery points and standing unmanaged privilege are outside appetite. Exceptions require named ownership, expiry, evidence, and board-visible residual risk.

## KRIs and thresholds

Quarterly report: Tier 0 tested recovery, phishing-resistant admin authentication, production-identity isolation, services lacking tested manual procedures, single-third-party dependence, privilege-disable time, clean-room establishment time, unreconciled transactions, exercises meeting tolerance, Tier 0 telemetry, critical findings, immutable backup coverage, and integrity-validated recovery points. Management proposes targets from tested baselines; the board approves thresholds. Until approved, dashboard status is “undefined,” never green.

## Treatment and prioritization

Compare accept, prevention, detection/response, redundancy, clean-room recovery, concentration reduction, financial transfer, and service redesign using cost, effort, risk reduction, dependencies, and residual risk. Controls include phishing-resistant MFA, privileged workstations/identities/PAM, identity tiers, management segmentation, independent recovery credentials, immutable/offline data, clean rooms, restoration tests, reconciliation, carrier diversity, exits, independent DNS/time, certificate and cloud monitoring, tamper-resistant logging, manual payments, and alternate crisis communications. No single control prevents capable compromise.

The qualitative and numeric decision matrices in `templates/` require narrative rationale; scores enable comparison and never decide funding.
